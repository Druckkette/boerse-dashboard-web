# Market Journal fonts

Unmodified TrueType files obtained from Google Fonts. They are served locally by
the dashboard; rendering the market view does not contact Google Fonts.

- DM Sans, normal 400/500/600 — `dmsans-OFL.txt`.
- IBM Plex Mono, normal 400/500 — `ibmplexmono-OFL.txt`.
- Instrument Serif, normal 400 — `instrumentserif-OFL.txt`.

Each family is licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. The adjacent license
files preserve the original copyright and reserved-font-name notices.

Upstream metadata and licenses: [DM Sans](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/dmsans),
[IBM Plex Mono](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/ibmplexmono),
[Instrument Serif](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/instrumentserif).
